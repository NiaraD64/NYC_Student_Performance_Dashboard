# NYC DOE Student Performance Analysis & Dashboard
This personal project visualizes NYC Department of Education (DOE) Math and English Language Arts (ELA) state assessment results for students in grades 3–8 from 2013–2023. The dashboard analyzes proficiency rates (students scoring at Levels 3 and 4, meeting or exceeding expectations) to explore performance trends over time and differences across geographic regions and student populations.

# Tools Used

-PostgreSQL (data preparation and cleaning)

-Tableau (data visualization and interactive dashboards)

# Key Tasks
-Performed data quality checks in PostgreSQL, including identifying duplicate records and missing values.

-Prepared and cleaned over 1 million NYC DOE assessment records for Tableau visualization.

-Developed interactive dashboards to explore Math and ELA proficiency trends from 2013–2023.

-Analyzed performance differences across grade levels, geographic regions, and student populations to explore educational equity trends.

# The Process

-Created two interactive Tableau dashboards to explore NYC student performance trends. 

- The first dashboard focuses on performance over time, while the second examines educational equity by comparing proficiency rates across geographic regions and student populations.

- Created calculated fields to support comparisons and develop key performance indicators.

# Key Findings
<img width="1662" height="822" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/991d45ec-572e-45d8-8945-4aa063863b74" />

-Math and ELA proficiency rates (students scoring at Levels 3 and 4) generally increased from 2013–2019. Following the disruption caused by COVID-19, ELA proficiency rates remained relatively stable from 2020–2023, while Math proficiency rates experienced a larger decline before increasing again in 2023.

-Grade-level comparisons showed differences in student performance. Across grades 3–8, 5th and 6th graders had the lowest average ELA proficiency rates at approximately 35%, while 8th graders had the lowest average Math proficiency rates at approximately 25%.

<img width="1827" height="922" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f9a7544-b6b6-4ba1-ac6c-df3ad5ee33a0" />

-Borough comparisons showed differences in proficiency rates, with students in the Bronx having the lowest average Level 3 and 4 proficiency rate at 26%, while Queens had the highest at 44%.

-ELL status comparisons revealed differences in student outcomes. Current ELL students had the lowest proficiency rate at 12%, while former ELL students had the highest at 48%.

-Economic status comparisons showed that economically disadvantaged students had a lower proficiency rate at 37% compared to students who were not economically disadvantaged at 52%.

-Race and ethnicity comparisons showed variation in proficiency rates, with Black students having the lowest average rate at 27% and Multiracial students having the highest at 66%.
